Open. Run. Give it a tilt.

Launch Hinge (for Windows), and turn it on from the system tray or settings. Set your comfortable open viewing position once, and Hinge remembers it for next time. Works with convertible and 2-in-1 hinge angle sensors, plus built-in interactive hotkeys and simulation.

Supports Windows.Devices.Sensors.HingeAngleSensor, HID hinge sensors, and virtual lid simulation.

A peek behind the bend.

Hinge samples your lid angle in real time. GPU acceleration gives your live desktop perspective and progressive Gaussian blur, with critically damped smoothing between readings. Tilt slowly, it follows slowly. Move faster, it keeps up.

Your screen stays strictly local in memory on your Windows PC. No recordings. No telemetry. No uploads.
